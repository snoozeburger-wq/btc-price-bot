4. HIGH RISK WORK ASSESSMENT &
CONTROLS
----------------------------------------
----------------------------------------
Job Step: 1. Site Arrival & Equipment
Unloading
Hazards: Traffic movement, manual
handling strains, slips/trips.
Control Measures: Park vehicles in
designated loading bays. Use team lifts
or mechanical aids for items over 20kg.
Wear high-visibility clothing.
Job Step: 2. Tool & Plant Inspection
Hazards: Electrocution, faulty power
tools, unguarded blades.
Control Measures: Inspect all power
tools before use. Verify Test & Tag
currency (3-month construction
standard). Ensure RCD protection is
active on power boards.
Job Step: 3. Working at Heights (if
applicable)
Hazards: Falls from ladders or
scaffolding, falling objects.
Control Measures: Inspect ladders
before use (industrial rated only).
Maintain 3 points of contact. Barricade
area below overhead work.
Job Step: 4. Site Clean-up & Waste
RemovalHazards: Puncture wounds from nails/
screws, environmental hazards.
Control Measures: Clean as you go.
Dispose of trade waste in designated
bins. Lock up all tools and hazardous
materials at end of shift.
